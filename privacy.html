Privacy policy

Tcipher Workspace · Last updated 12 November 2026

Tcipher Workspace is the internal work app of Tcipher Technologies. Only people with an account created by Tcipher’s admin can sign in. This page explains what the app does with your data, including data from your Google account if you choose to connect it.

What we store

  • Your name, work email, job title and phone number, as entered by you or an admin.
  • The work you add: tasks, comments, notes, files, meetings, leads, deals, leave and attendance.

Google Calendar and Google Meet (optional)

If you click “Connect Google”, you allow the app to:

  • See your email address, to show which Google account is connected.
  • Read and manage your calendar events (calendar.events): your events from the past 7 days and the next 45 days are copied into the workspace so your team calendar and Home page show your meetings with Join buttons. When you create a meeting in the workspace, it’s added to your Google Calendar with a Google Meet link and invitations.
  • Read Google Meet attendance (meetings.space.readonly): for meetings you organise, the app reads who joined and when, to log attendance (for example, the morning scrum).
  • Send email from your Gmail (gmail.send): when you click Send in the workspace, the email goes from your own address to the people you chose.
  • Read replies and emails from leads (gmail.readonly): the app opens the conversations that were started from the workspace, to show the replies on the right lead, deal, client or person. For new emails in your inbox it reads only the sender’s address, to spot emails from a lead or deal contact saved in the workspace; only those conversations are copied to that record. It doesn’t store anything else from your mailbox.
  • See files Google Meet created (drive.meet.readonly, only if you allow it from a meeting): for meetings you organise in the workspace, the app copies the text of Gemini’s meeting notes into the meeting’s notes and links the recording and transcript. It can’t see other files in your Drive.

Your Google events are visible to signed-in Tcipher colleagues in the workspace. Emails about a lead, deal, client, contact or proposal are visible to colleagues who can open that record; emails to employees (such as offer letters) are visible only to the sender and admins. Your Google access token is stored encrypted on our server (Supabase) and is never shown in anyone’s browser.

Booking a demo (for people outside Tcipher)

If you book a demo on our booking page, we store the name, email, phone, company and note you enter, with the time you picked, so our team can prepare and contact you about the demo. Your email receives a Google Calendar invite from the Tcipher team member taking the call. The page shows only free times, never anyone’s calendar. To have your details removed, reply to the invite or email us.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell, rent or share your data with anyone outside Tcipher Technologies.
  • We don’t use Google data for advertising, and we don’t use it to train AI models.
  • We only use Google data to provide the features described above.

The app’s use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect Google at any time in the workspace (your name › My profile › Disconnect). This deletes your stored Google token and your copied calendar events, and stops email sending and reply checks. You can also remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Where data is kept

Workspace data is stored with Supabase (a database and storage provider) in its Mumbai region, and the app is served from GitHub Pages.
